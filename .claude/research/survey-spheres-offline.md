# Survey: sphere representation and the shading-normal problem in offline renderers

Date of survey: 2026-10-06. Sources are official documentation, release notes, developer
forum answers and open source where available. Each row is labelled:

- **Fact**: stated by the cited source.
- **Inference**: my reading of a source that does not say it in those words.
- **Unconfirmed**: searched for and not found in a citable source.

Legend for the three questions:

- Q1: analytic sphere primitive, or always a mesh?
- Q2: smooth shading default, and how reflection and shadow rays from a shading normal that
  dips below the geometric surface are handled (the "shading normal problem" / "shadow
  terminator").
- Q3: what the vendor recommends for a perfect sphere.

## Techniques, with sources

1. **Shading normals and their non-physical consequences.** Veach 1997, thesis chapter 5 ("The sources of non-symmetric scattering"), §5.3: shading normals make the BSDF non-symmetric and can reflect energy below the surface; the correction factor |ω·Ns| / |ω·Ng| is derived there. https://graphics.stanford.edu/papers/veach_thesis/ (chapter 5).
2. **Bend the reflection back above the surface** ("valid reflection"). Iray: Keller et al. 2017, arXiv 1705.01263, appendix A.3 "Local Shading Normal Adaption" (quoted above). Cycles: `ensure_valid_reflection()`, Stockner 2018 (commit 65b25df8), applied to all BSDF normals since Blender 3.0 (commit ca0450fe). Redshift: "Keller" and "Skewed Reflection" options. V-Ray: Vlado's 2010 description of the problem; the fix is unnamed.
3. **Microfacet-based normal mapping.** Schüssler, Heitz, Hanika, Dachsbacher 2017, ACM TOG 36(6) (SIGGRAPH Asia): a two-facet microsurface whose average normal equals the geometric normal; symmetric and energy conserving. https://cg.ivd.kit.edu/publications/2017/normalmaps/normalmap.pdf . No renderer above documents adopting it.
4. **Shadowing-function softening of the terminator.** Chiang, Li, Burley 2019 "Taming the Shadow Terminator" (SIGGRAPH Talks): https://media.disneyanimation.com/technology/publications/2019/TamingtheShadowTerminator.pdf . Estevez, Lecocq, Stein 2019, "A Microfacet-Based Shadowing Function to Solve the Bump Terminator Problem", Ray Tracing Gems ch. 12 (Sony Imageworks): https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-1-4842-4427-2_12.pdf . Deshmukh and Green 2020 (DreamWorks), "Predictable and Targeted Softening of the Shadow Terminator": https://research.dreamworks.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/talk_shadow_terminator.pdf . Cycles implements Chiang et al. (commit c3be14e). RenderMan 24 "Bump Shadow Terminator" and Karma "Bump Shadow Terminator" are the same class of fix, algorithm unpublished.
5. **Move the shading point to a smooth-surface estimate** (offset the shadow/secondary ray origin). Hanika 2021, "Hacking the Shadow Terminator", Ray Tracing Gems II ch. 4, which traces the idea to Hanika's 2011 thesis §5.2.9 and relates it to quadratic Bézier (PN-triangle) patches: https://jo.dreggn.org/home/2021_terminator.pdf . Cycles "Geometry Offset" (commit 9c6a382) is this idea. Mantra's `vm_smoothP` ("smooth P") is, by its name and the staff description, the same class (Inference). Redshift's Shadow Ray Biasing is an adaptive epsilon of the same family.
6. **Analytic spheres.** Mantra quadric primitives; Arnold `sphere` and `points mode sphere`; Cycles point clouds (Embree sphere primitive on CPU); RenderMan RIS quadrics (not XPU); V-Ray `VRaySphere`; Redshift Optimized Spheres (particles only); Karma points "Render Points As: sphere". None of the vendors ships a shading-normal fix for an analytic sphere because the shading normal equals the geometric normal there.

## Gaps left open

- Arnold: the per-node `sphere` reference page and the published algorithm of its shadow terminator fix were not found. Request's "Normal flip" / `indirect_specular` as terminator controls: not confirmed.
- RenderMan: the algorithm behind "polygon shadow terminators" (24.0) is not published.
- Karma: no documented technique for plain interpolated normals without bump; no page states whether a SOP quadric sphere becomes a USD `Sphere` or a mesh on import.
- V-Ray: the VRaySphere docs page content comes from a search snippet; the page itself redirected.
- Blender issue #68920 (developer discussion of the chosen technique) returned 403 and was not read.
