**Decision 0008.22.** The panel lists these controls by class: `color`, `emissive`, `emissiveIntensity` and `doubleSided`. The `emissive` control shows the hue of the emission and the `emissiveIntensity` control shows its intensity. The panel derives both from the radiance, and only `PhysicalMaterial` has a field `emissiveIntensity`. `PhysicalMaterial`'s five parameters wait for step 2 of record 0004, and textures for step 3. Proposed: yes.
