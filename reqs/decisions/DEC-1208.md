**Decision 0012.8.** **Rectangle and disk lights are invisible to rays.** Proposed: yes, as record 0010 decision 17 for point lights. A light that a ray must see is an emissive mesh. The alternative makes each shape visible. That needs an analytic intersection of a disk and a rectangle in the kernel. It is a second change of record 0001.
