**Decision 0001.15.** `SphereGeometry` stays a triangle mesh with smooth vertex normals by default, and a glTF mesh keeps the normals of its file. The Cornell box uses two `Sphere` objects. The `geometries` example keeps its mesh sphere. A new example, `spheres`, compares a smooth mesh ball, a mesh ball with `flatShading` and a `Sphere`. This is the owner's decision of 2026-10-06 (final). Amendment 3 adds this decision.
