**Decision 0001.10.** The analytic sphere is a separate shape kind named `Sphere`, as pbrt and Mitsuba name it. It is not a mode of `SphereGeometry`. A ray meets it by the quadratic. The hit is exact, and `ng` and `ns` are equal. This is the owner's decision of 2026-10-06 (final). Amendment 3 adds this decision.
