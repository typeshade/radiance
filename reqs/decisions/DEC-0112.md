**Decision 0001.12.** The host object is `Sphere(radius, material)`, an `Object3D` beside `Mesh`. A non-uniform world scale or a shear is refused with a `RangeError` that names the three axis lengths. The kernel does not intersect in the sphere's own space, and it draws no ellipsoid. The owner asked the record to decide this. Decided by default. Amendment 3 adds this decision.
