**Decision 0010.5.** For the owner. The integer words of `materials` are the values of `f32` numbers below 2^24, and the engine keeps seven storage buffers. Decision 8 of record 0004 left this choice to the owner, between this and an eighth buffer. A texture id is `0` for none, else `1 + class * 256 + layer`. Proposed: this choice.
