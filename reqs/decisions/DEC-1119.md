**Decision 0011.19.** A physical surfel hit is a surface: it goes through `sampleBsdf`, next-event estimation and MIS, and receives the scene's lights and mesh shadows. A baked hit adds its radiance and ends the path. A shadow ray stops at an accepted Gaussian of either mode. Proposed: yes. This asks the owner, because it fixes what a splat can show.
