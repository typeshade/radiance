**Decision 0011.31.** A decomposition can fail one of the three checks. The result then keeps the materials, uses baked shading for the whole geometry, and reports the check. The user may override. A mix of physical and baked surfels in one instance is not proposed. Proposed: yes. This asks the owner, because it decides what a user gets on a failure.
