**Decision 0012.2.** **Denoising directly after M3.** Proposed: yes. The denoiser is the largest gain at 64 to 256 samples per pixel (plan section 6), and it reads the AOVs of M3. Its goldens are not affected by Parts 1 to 5 once it is measured. The cost: Parts 1 to 5 change pictures after the denoiser's goldens exist. The owner accepts that cost with this order.
