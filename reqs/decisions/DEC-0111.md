**Decision 0001.11.** A `Sphere` is stored as one instance whose `flags` bit 0 is set. Its words hold the centre and the radius in world space, the rotation for the uv, and the material. The BVH holds it as a TLAS leaf entry, with no node in a BLAS ("The analytic sphere"). Decided by default. Amendment 3 adds this decision.
