**Decision 0011.11.** Training has one mode. It is bit-identical on one device and driver for one seed. Across devices and tiers it is in a PSNR band of the WebGPU run. The band's width is the larger of 0.5 dB and three standard deviations of the held-out PSNR over 8 WebGPU seeds at the same budget. Training kernels are exempt from rule 2 (Amendment D). No fast mode now. Proposed: yes. This asks the owner, because it changes the scope of record 0005.
