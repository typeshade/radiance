**Decision 0008.9.** The field `normal` of an `Intersection` is the unit geometric normal in world space. three.js's `face.normal` is in object space. Step 1 checks whether three.js's `Intersection` has a `normal` with another meaning. If it has, an amendment renames the field `faceNormal`. Proposed: yes.
