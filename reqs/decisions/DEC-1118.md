**Decision 0011.18.** The path tracer's splat rules: a ray accepts each Gaussian by a hash with the chance of its alpha. The falloff is a polynomial within 1e-6 of `exp(-q / 2)`. `Q_MAX` is 9, `ALPHA_MIN` is 1/255, `ALPHA_MAX` is 0.99. Proposed: yes. This is a default.
