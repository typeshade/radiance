**Decision 0002.6.** The analytic sphere is held by instruments that do not share its pack: an `f64` reference, the goldens, and the radius probes. The differential gate alone cannot hold it. A scene `spheres` joins the differential scenes, and the Cornell box's spheres become analytic. Decided by default. Amendment 5 adds this decision.
