**Decision 0011.7.** The ingest reads MP4 and MOV with a reader written in the package, and decodes with WebCodecs. The alternative is one dependency, mp4box.js, which needs a change of the boundary. Proposed: the package's own reader. This asks the owner, because the other answer changes the boundary.
