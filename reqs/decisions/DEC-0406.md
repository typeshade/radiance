**Decision 0004.6.** The rules that Amendment 1 writes for the surface and for emission: a back face emits only for a material with bit 9, `Surface.p` is offset by `OFFSET` times the largest of 1 and the point's largest absolute coordinate, `ns` falls back to `ng`, and the path loop ends a path whose direction goes under `ng`.
