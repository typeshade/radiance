**Decision 0009.15.** The sample budget `L` is `ceil(log2(maxSamples))`, at most 16, and 10 when `maxSamples` is infinite. A render past the budget starts a new epoch. A frame wider than a block uses independent blocks. Proposed: yes. This is a default.
