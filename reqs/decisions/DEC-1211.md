**Decision 0012.11.** **Photometric units.** `KHR_lights_punctual` gives candela and lux. The engine uses the radiometric units of record 0010 decision 17. Proposed: the loader keeps the number with no conversion. A loader option `photometric` converts with 683 lumens per watt, for a point light and for a sun. The default is no conversion. The owner decides the default.
