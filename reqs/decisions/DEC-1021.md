**Decision 0010.21.** Default. `PhysicalCamera` extends `PerspectiveCamera`. The exposure is a number of stops, added on the host. Its default `relative` mode adds 0 stops at the defaults, and the `absolute` mode follows Lagarde and de Rousiers. Proposed: yes.
