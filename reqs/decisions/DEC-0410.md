**Decision 0004.10.** A material has a `flatShading` flag, default false, in bit 11 of `[2].w` (byte 45, mask 0x08). When it is true, the kernel and the oracle shade with `ns` equal to `ng`, as three.js's `material.flatShading` does. This is the owner's decision of 2026-10-06 (final). Amendment 3 adds this decision.
