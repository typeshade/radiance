**Decision 0012.9.** **The environment's spherical harmonics are not used.** The path tracer samples the environment, so the irradiance of `EXT_lights_image_based` is not needed. Proposed: yes. The alternative stores the nine coefficients and uses them as a diffuse-only fallback, which the path tracer does not need.
