**Decision 0010.24.** For the owner. The geometry AOVs have an entry, `aov`, and a buffer, `aovAccum`, with six storage bindings. The light groups use a larger `accum` stride. The `trace` pipeline keeps seven. At 1920 by 1080 the stride is at most 4. Proposed: yes.
