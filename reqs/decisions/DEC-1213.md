**Decision 0012.13.** **Radial distortion only.** The full model is the Brown-Conrady model with `k1`, `k2`, `k3`, `p1` and `p2`. Proposed: radial `k1` and `k2` now, and the full model as a follow-on step with the same gate. The reason: tangential terms and `k3` are rarely set in a product shot.
