**Decision 0011.6.** The read formats are the INRIA `.ply`, the 2DGS `.ply` with the material properties, `.splat`, `.spz` versions 2 and 3, and `KHR_gaussian_splatting`. The written formats are the 2DGS `.ply` and the glTF extension. `.spz` version 4, SOG, the compressed `.ply` and `.ksplat` wait. Proposed: yes. This asks the owner, because it sets the scope.
