**Decision 0012.12.** **Trigonometry for panoramic directions.** Amendment H of record 0005 admits `sin` and `cos` of an angle from `turn()` when the value is a direction. Proposed: yes. The alternative keeps the rule and uses a polynomial of the same angle, which is less accurate near the poles.
