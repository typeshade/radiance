**Decision 0011.14.** Adjoints accumulate as fixed-point integers: two `u32` words with sign extension by default, one `i32` word where a bound is proven. Other tiers add the same integers by a sorted gather or a loop. Subgroup pre-reduction is an optional speed path after C8. Proposed: yes. This asks the owner, because it amends rules 2, 4 and 6 of record 0005.
