**Decision 0011.14.** Adjoints accumulate as fixed-point integers by `atomicAdd` on `i32`, or on two `u32` words where the range needs 64 bits. Other tiers add the same integers by a sorted gather or a loop. Subgroup pre-reduction is an optional speed path after C8. Proposed: yes. This asks the owner, because it amends rules 4 and 6 of record 0005.
