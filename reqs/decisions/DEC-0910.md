**Decision 0009.10.** The accumulator has at most 8 buckets. The G-MoN output needs at least 4. A frame over 2,097,152 pixels has fewer than 4 buckets, so it gets the plain mean only. A frame over 1,048,576 pixels gets fewer than 8. Proposed: yes. This asks the owner, because it costs memory.
