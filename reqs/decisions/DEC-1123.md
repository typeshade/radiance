**Decision 0011.23.** The baked colour above degree 0 is stored as `f16` pairs. If the probe of step 1.1 fails, it is `f32` bits in `triangles`, 192 bytes a Gaussian at degree 3, and a binding holds 699,050 Gaussians. Proposed: `f16` pairs. This is a default.
