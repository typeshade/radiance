**Decision 0010.19.** Default. An environment is an equirectangular `rgba32float` image of at most 2,048 texels across. Its distribution is at most 1,024 across. The rotation is about +y only. `scene.background` takes a `Color`, an `Environment` or `null`. Proposed: yes.
